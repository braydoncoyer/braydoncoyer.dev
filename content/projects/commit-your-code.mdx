## About

The Commit Your Code Conference is a two-day annual event where leading experts in various web development fields come together to speak. The conference is organized by volunteers who dedicate their time. All proceeds, including ticket sales and brand sponsorships, are donated directly to charity.

After the inaugural event, I, as one of the organizers, was tasked with establishing a brand design and creating a website for the 2025 conference.

The goal was to establish a brand that was easily recognizable, and create the best tech-related conference site in the world.
